How to use
  1. Click "Go to Safeway Coupons" to open the deals page.
  2. Wait for the coupons to finish loading.
  3. Click the green ✂ Clip All Coupons button that appears in the bottom-right corner of the page.
  4. The extension will automatically scroll and clip every available coupon.
  5. Click the button again at any time to cancel.
