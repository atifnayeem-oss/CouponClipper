How to use

1
Drag the green button to your bookmarks bar Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows/Linux, Cmd+Shift+B on Mac). Then drag the button above into it.
2
Go to safeway.com and log into your account You need to be signed in so the coupons get added to your loyalty card.
3
Navigate to Coupons & Deals, then click "Clip All Coupons" Click the bookmarklet in your bookmarks bar while on safeway.com and sit back — it does the rest automatically.

What happens when you click it

  • A green progress panel appears in the bottom-right corner of safeway.com
  • It automatically scrolls through the coupons page and clicks every unclipped coupon
  • A live counter shows how many coupons have been clipped so far
  • Click Cancel anytime to stop — your progress is kept
